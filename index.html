D_LLM_v1 Terms and Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. About the Application

D_LLM_v1 is an academic research prototype developed for research involving health information, human-computer interaction, and large language model technologies.

2. Access to Health Information

D_LLM_v1 uses SMART on FHIR and OAuth 2.0 to allow individuals to authorize access to their health information from compatible Epic-based health systems.

Users explicitly authorize the application through the health system's authorization workflow before the application can access their information.

3. Information Accessed

The application accesses only the information authorized by the user through the SMART on FHIR authorization process.

For the current prototype, this may include basic patient demographic information such as name, date of birth, and gender.

4. Research Use

Information accessed through the application is used for academic research and prototype development purposes.

5. No Medical Advice

D_LLM_v1 is a research prototype and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. The application is not intended to replace professional medical care or clinical systems.

6. User Authorization

Users may choose whether to authorize the application to access their information. Users may discontinue use of the application at any time.
